## WHY DO EYELASH EXTENSIONS FALL? AND HOW SHOULD WE TAKE CARE OF THEM? 
 The eyelash extension is the most requested and fashionable luxury treatment.
 Eight out of ten women declare that thanks to the extension they feel more beautiful and comfortable, and they would like to insert it as a stable habit in the routine for their psychophysical well-being and the care of their image.
Even though it is still considered a luxury treatment, many women want eyelash extension treatment. That is for practical reasons and to accomplish the need to feel attractive and seductive.
 In today’s article, we will understand why eyelash extensions can fall and how to take care of them.
 There are two factors why eyelashes can fall.   
 The first is for natural causes (customers):
 - Greasy eyelashes;
 - Hormonal disorders;
 - Medicines (cortisone, antibiotics, psychiatric drugs, vitamins, alcohol, and menstrual cycle);
 - Excessive use of makeup;
 - Lack of daily cleansing of eyelashes (we recommend NeoLash® shampoo specific for eyelashes.).
 The second is for factors depending either on the environment or Lash Artist:
 - Excessive (or too little) humidity in the room;
- High or too-low temperatures;
 - A glue not suitable for the Lash Artist’s speed of workflow;
 - A wrong cleansing at the beginning of treatment, etc.
 Well, now that we understand why eyelash extensions can fall, let’s see how to take care of and cleanse them.
## HOW TO CLEANSE EYELASH EXTENSIONS
 [img IMG_20221205_111652_475-200x300.jpg] 
 Wash hands thoroughly and remove any contact lenses;
 Shake the NeoLash® shampoo before use and take 1 or 2 doses of eyelash cleanser;
 In the morning and evening, apply it on your hands or a brush and make light movements on your eyelashes with your eyes closed;
 Rinse with plenty of water and dry the eyelashes with a paper towel, then comb.
